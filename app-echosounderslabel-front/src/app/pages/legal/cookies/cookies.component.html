Cookies

 1. What are cookies? Cookies and similar storage technologies are small files stored in your browser that allow a website to remember information about your visit. 2. Cookies used on this website This website does not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. Only technical storage mechanisms (localStorage) are used to: Prevent multiple form submissions within a limited period of time Remember previously entered form data to improve user experience These mechanisms are strictly necessary for the proper functioning of the website. 3. Purpose of the cookies The stored data is used exclusively to: Detect whether a demo has already been submitted Automatically refill form fields with previously entered data No personal data is shared with third parties or used for profiling. 4. Legal basis According to Article 22.2 of the Spanish Law on Information Society Services (LSSI-CE) and GDPR, technical and strictly necessary cookies do not require explicit consent. 5. Duration The stored data remains in your browser until it is manually deleted or expires automatically after a defined period. 6. How to delete cookies You can delete stored data at any time through your browser settings. 7. Contact If you have any questions about this Cookies Policy, you can contact: Andrea Rodríguez
